# ONE GOOD PART — los 40 prompts de ilustración

Hoja de producción. Cada prompt final = **BLOQUE A + BLOQUE B + la escena
numerada**, en ese orden, pegados en un solo texto.

## Reglas de la herramienta (medidas, no supuestas)

1. **`design_type: "poster"` y nada más.** `facebook_post` devuelve una plantilla
   de marketing con título encima y `reallygreatsite.com` quemado en el pixel.
   Las internas 4:3 se generan también en poster (1587×2245) y se recortan al
   montar.
2. **Ruleta de candidatos.** Cada llamada devuelve 4, y con el MISMO prompt uno
   puede salir plantilla de rebajas y otro ilustración limpia. Nunca tomar el
   candidato 1 a ciegas: exportar, mirar, y recién ahí elegir.
3. **Cuota agresiva.** Pasan ~2 generaciones seguidas y luego `quota limit`.
   Se repone en ~24 h.
4. **Verificar cada imagen abriéndola**, no por el título del diseño. El título lo
   escribe Canva y miente.
5. **El ambiente hay que forzarlo como sujeto.** Canva trata como decorativo todo
   lo que no sea el personaje: un "flooded floor, ankle-deep water" salió como un
   charquito sobre madera seca. Cuando el ambiente ES la historia (la inundación,
   la niebla, el agua de la laguna), va descrito con tres frases y con el
   personaje interactuando físicamente con él.
6. **El "no text" se filtra.** Igual mete papeles enmarcados con garabatos.
   Pedir explícitamente paredes sin documentos ni carteles enmarcados.

## BLOQUE A — style token

> Full-bleed book illustration, edge to edge artwork. STYLE: cinematic cel-shaded
> animated feature background art in the tradition of Studio Ghibli and Cartoon
> Saloon — clean confident shapes with soft painted lighting, visible shafts of
> light cutting through dust in the air, warm lamp glow against cool blue shadow,
> rich atmospheric depth, layered foreground and background, muted natural
> palette, expressive but never messy. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, signs
> or writing anywhere in the image; every sign board, label and poster in the
> scene must be completely blank.

## BLOQUE B — personajes

> CHARACTERS, keep exactly consistent in every image: Professor Halden Rooke is a
> thin man in his mid-seventies with wild shoulder-length white hair, a short
> white beard, small round wire glasses and deep lines around his eyes. Nia
> Serrano is a seventeen-year-old girl with light brown skin, straight dark brown
> hair in a high ponytail, a white shirt with rolled sleeves and an olive-green
> canvas backpack.

**Regla de conteo:** toda escena con personas declara cuántas hay. Si no, Canva
duplica al protagonista — ya pasó una vez.

## Capítulo 1 · Lisboa 2026

**01 · apertura** — SCENE: a narrow cobbled street in the Alfama district of Lisbon at dawn, tiled facades, iron balconies and tram rails set into the stones. **Exactly one person in the whole picture: Nia, alone.** She stands at the door of a small ground-floor repair workshop, key in her hand, as a sheet of water runs out from under the door across the cobblestones toward her shoes. Cool blue morning light in the street, one warm bulb glowing inside through the glass. The shopfront sign board above the door is blank and unpainted.

**02 · pág. 2** — SCENE: interior of a flooded repair workshop, ankle-deep water across the floor reflecting the ceiling lamps. Close view of a teenage girl's hands, and only her hands and forearms, lifting a soaked cardboard box out of the water; the wet cardboard is coming apart and cassette tapes slide out of it back into the water. Warm lamplight above, cool blue shadows, tools and paper floating behind.

**03 · pág. 3** — SCENE: interior of a cluttered repair workshop with water standing on the floor. **Exactly one person: Rooke, alone.** Barefoot in a rumpled shirt, he pulls a heavy grey canvas tarpaulin off something tall against the back wall; the corner of a large metal frame is just beginning to show underneath. Dust turning in a shaft of light from a high window.

**04 · pág. 4** — SCENE: extreme close-up of one small brass gear wheel lying on a wet wooden workbench, a clear crack running across its teeth. A desk lamp throws a warm pool of light onto it; water beads on the bench and small hand tools lie out of focus around it. **No people.** The rest of the room is dark.

## Capítulo 2 · Kioto 1889

**05 · apertura** — SCENE: **exactly two people, Rooke and Nia, and no one else.** They step out of a narrow lane into a wider street in Kyoto at first light in 1889, both wearing borrowed dark period jackets. Two-storey wooden machiya houses with paper windows line both sides, one gas lamp still burning at the far end, cold blue pre-dawn mist low over the road. Seen from behind and slightly to the side. Every shop banner is blank.

**06 · pág. 2** — SCENE: a street food stall in Kyoto at dawn in 1889, thick steam rising from an iron pot into the cold air. **Exactly three people:** Rooke and Nia stand at the counter holding hot bowls with both hands, breath visible; the stall keeper leans over the pot laughing. Warm orange lantern light against the blue morning street behind. The stall's cloth banner is blank.

**07 · pág. 3** — SCENE: a wide dirt street in Kyoto in 1889, early morning. A crew of workmen in short indigo jackets and headbands haul on ropes to raise a tall wooden pole for a new electric tram cable, bodies leaning back with the strain, while an ox cart loaded with sacks passes behind them without slowing. Low sun, dust in the air, long shadows.

**08 · pág. 4** — SCENE: interior of a small metalworking workshop in Kyoto in 1889, seen from floor level. **Exactly one person:** a young Japanese craftsman of about twenty kneeling at a low wooden bench, bent close over a tiny gear wheel he is filing by lamplight, completely absorbed. In the foreground, on the raised entrance step, two pairs of worn shoes left neatly side by side. Pale morning light through a paper screen door.

## Control

40 prompts · 10 aperturas a página completa · 30 internas 4:3 · 6 ciudades ·
7 épocas. Personajes con conteo declarado en las 24 escenas con gente; las otras
16 son objetos, manos o lugares vacíos — que además son las más seguras de
generar, porque Canva no puede equivocarse en la cara de nadie.
